## Reachability and non-vacuity

- `keypair_internal_mode2_return_prefix` supplies the prefix relation for every
  terminating mode-2 internal KeyGen call.
- `keygen_prefix_reaches_mu_memory` is retained as a historical Week 2 local
  witness only.  No Week 5 API-level theorem uses it or any `stores mem 0 ...`
  construction.
- `mode2_base_zero_no_wrap` gives a concrete satisfiable address-range witness.
- `generated_raw_mu_preconditions_from_keygen_prefix` jointly witnesses
  KeyGen-derived `sk_memory_prefix`, base-zero no-wrap, and `raw_prelen`; the
  direct generated theorem's premises are therefore non-contradictory.
- `keypair_internal_return_reaches_generated_raw_mu_preconditions` supplies
  that relation from the actual generated mode-2 internal KeyGen return under
  Hoare partial correctness; it still makes no termination claim.
- The four generated API copy-helper theorems establish their exact isolated
  byte behavior under satisfiable `valid_region_w64`/`valid_region_int`
  predicates. `mode2_api_region_contract_satisfiable` supplies a concrete
  adjacent-region witness, and `keygen_export_sk_mode2_prefix_frames_vk`
  proves the disjoint write frame.
- Week 5 closes the actual raw KeyGen sequential export postcondition and the
  exact raw/cryptolab Verify trace lifts.  The early-reject proof separately
  establishes `accept => tail_reached`, and the accepted branch binds the
  actual descriptor values supplied to `__verify_hash_mu`.
- `raw_api_accepting_execution_hash_mu_zero_loss` and
  `raw_api_accepting_execution_generated_challenge_suffix_zero_loss` are
  non-vacuous generated-call adapters whose premises are supplied by the
  exact caller traces.  They do not yet compare the two trace modules'
  `observed_mu` fields or prove that Sign output writes frame the VK/SK
  regions across calls.
- `raw_prelen_shr63_zero` and `raw_prelen_branch_guard` prove that the actual
  Verify guard selects the raw branch; `ctxflag=0` is not postulated.
- `OBL-MU-TOPLEVEL-CONTROL` is closed for raw/internal calls.  The former
  `OBL-API-KEY-MEMORY-RAW` is explicitly split: its accepted-path child
  `OBL-API-KEY-MEMORY-RAW-ACCEPT` remains **PARTIAL**. Week 6 closes its
  Sign-output frame and region-local generated-hash children and compiles the
  actual sequential trace, but not the direct relation between the two stored
  `observed_mu` values. Legitimate Sign output tail reach and full Sign/Verify
  correctness remain separate named functional obligations. Consequently no
  `delta_mu_raw_api_accept = 0` claim is recorded.

  low arrays satisfying the exact canonical predicates used by
  `pack_unpack_sig_prefix_mode2_roundtrip`. Thus the new prefix theorem is not
  vacuous. It remains partial correctness and does not supply a suffix,
  accepted signature, norm-gate, or signing-termination witness.
