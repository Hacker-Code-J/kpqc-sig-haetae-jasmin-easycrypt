\begin{abstract}
This document freezes the strongest paper-safe results that are actually
fresh-compiled for the pinned HAETAE-2 Jasmin implementation.  The proved
spine includes exact transcript and caller-boundary lemmas, a mode-2
signature-codec vertical slice through the HBZ and rANS stack, the actual
KeyGen M23 matrix/finalizer snapshot with KG-2 decomposition and a
snapshot-only congruence modulo \(2q\), and the actual Verify helper chain
with V-1, V-2, V-5, V-6, the norm gate, and the tail mismatch path.  The
paper is intentionally frozen short of stronger claims: the faithful KeyGen
equation \(A s = q j \pmod{2q}\), paper-literal Sign S-1--S-7, Verify V-3/V-4,
and any full Sign\(\Rightarrow\)Verify theorem all remain blocked by named
missing leaves.  The final freeze section states those exact blockers,
preserves the Week~16 82-target reproduction boundary, and records the trusted
computing base; the chronological sections that follow remain as the audit
trail for how that boundary was reached.
\end{abstract}
