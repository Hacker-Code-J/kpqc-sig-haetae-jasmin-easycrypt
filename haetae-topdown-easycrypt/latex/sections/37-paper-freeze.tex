\subsection*{Main achievements}

\begin{itemize}
\item The project now proves direct actual-procedure refinement statements
  rather than paper-only surrogates at the transcript seam, raw/internal
  caller boundary, and accepted Verify helper chain.
\item The signature-codec vertical slice is mechanically closed through the
  mode-2 prefix pack/unpack round trip, HBZ leaf inverse, concrete table
  certificate, exact decoder refinement, and the success-conditioned actual
  rANS core/wrapper composition.
\item KeyGen is frozen at an honest actual-procedure finalization boundary:
  the M23 matrix/finalizer harness exports exact snapshots and semantic
  low/high decomposition facts without relabelling them as the paper key
  equation.
\item Verify now has an explicit proved reconstruction/norm frontier:
  helper-local V-1, V-2, V-5, V-6, the norm gate, and the tail mismatch path
  are all compiled against the actual helper order.
\item The missing NTT/CRT ingredients are now named as concrete checked leaf
  gaps rather than informal future work.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Explicit non-claims}

\begin{itemize}
\item No theorem in this paper claims \(A s = q j \pmod{2q}\).
\item No theorem in this paper claims paper-literal S-1--S-7.
\item No theorem in this paper claims paper-literal V-3 or V-4.
\item No theorem in this paper claims full Sign\(\Rightarrow\)Verify
  correctness, full public-API correctness, or decoded-object composition.
\item No theorem in this paper claims sampler distribution, retry/loop
  termination, malformed-input rejection, or EUF-CMA security.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Exact blocker leaf hierarchy}

\paragraph{KeyGen.}
The blocked paper conclusion
\[
  A s = q j \pmod{2q}
\]
first needs a checked identification of
\breakablesame{KeygenM23ArithmeticSpec.output_row} with the security-model row
product.  That bridge requires the missing leaf
\breakablett{full_ntt_montgomery_spectral_action}; its proof in turn needs the
256-term odd-root orthogonality identity.  Even after that native array-level
theorem, the checked tree still lacks the adapter from \breakablett{Rq.poly}
to the integer-list multiplication used by
\breakablesame{HAETAE_Algebra.poly_mul} and \breakablesame{matrix_vec_mul}.

\paragraph{Sign.}
The stopped headline theorem is
\breakablett{actual_sign_accepted_core_equations_mode2}.  Its first absent
Sign-specific semantic leaf is
\breakablett{sf_challenge_mode2_highbits_lsb_sampleinball_correct}, which must
connect actual highbits, packed LSBs of rounded \(y_0\), the first 32 bytes of
\(\mu\), and mode-2 \(\mathsf{SampleInBall}(\cdots,58)\).  Independently,
paper S-1 and S-4 retain the frozen KeyGen NTT-convolution/adapter dependency;
S-5 and S-6 need
\breakablett{_sf_add_cs_and_check_mode2_integer_semantics}; and S-7 needs
\breakablett{_sf_hint_mode2_hint_equation_correct}.

\paragraph{Verify.}
The planned headline theorem
\breakablesame{Mode2VerifyCorePredicate.actual_verify_core_predicate_mode2} cannot
start until the combined bridge
\breakablett{verify_matrix_crt_mode2_fromcrt_freeze_exact} exists.  That bridge
splits into two absent procedural leaves:
\breakablett{verify_matrix_ntt_acc_mode2_cols4_correct} and
\breakablett{verify_crt_freeze_mode2_word_exact}.  The NTT leaf first encounters
the pure normalization gap \breakablett{rq_mul_coeff_foldr_to_bigi}, then the
same missing \breakablett{full_ntt_montgomery_spectral_action}, then the same
odd-root orthogonality identity.  Downstream residuals remain the V-6 integer
centering bridge, the norm no-wrap bridge, and the challenge leaf
\breakablett{verify_tail_m23_highbits_lsb_sampleinball_correct}.
