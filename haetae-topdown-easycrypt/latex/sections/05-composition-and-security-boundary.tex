\section{Composition and Security Boundary}
\label{sec:week2-composition}

\paragraph{Target chain.}
The first seam is:
\[
\prefix_{992}(sk)=vk
\Rightarrow \mathsf{equal\_hash\_inputs}
\Rightarrow \take_{32}(\mu_{\mathsf{sign}}^{64})
             =\mu_{\mathsf{verify}}^{32}
\Rightarrow \mathsf{equal\_challenge\_suffix}.
\]

\paragraph{Reachability from KeyGen.}
\theoryname{keygen\_prefix\_reaches\_mu\_memory} consumes the actual
\theoryname{vk\_prefix\_eq} returned by the generated KeyGen theorem and
constructs
\[
  mem'=\mathsf{stores}\bigl(mem,\mathsf{to\_uint}(base),
        \take_{992}(\mathsf{to\_list}(vk))\bigr).
\]
It proves the exact \theoryname{sk\_memory\_prefix} premise required by the mu
hash theorem. The independent lemma
\theoryname{mode2\_base\_zero\_no\_wrap} proves that \(base=0\) satisfies the
992-byte address-range condition. Together these are a concrete
non-vacuity/reachability argument, not an existential witness over unrelated
mu arrays.

\paragraph{Security-facing composition theorem.}
\theoryname{raw\_mu\_to\_challenge\_suffix\_zero\_loss} compares two composed
programs:
\[
\begin{split}
 &\mathsf{SignRawMuTop};\
   \procname{\_\_sign\_challenge\_shake256\_absorb\_mu32},\\
 &\mathsf{VerifyRawMuTop};\
   \procname{\_\_verify\_shake256\_absorb\_mu32}.
\end{split}
\]
In addition to the raw mu premises of Section~\ref{sec:week2-mu}, it requires
equal initial challenge states/pointers and position 64. Its postcondition is
equality of the returned challenge state and position. The proof composes
\theoryname{sign\_verify\_raw\_mu\_top\_wrappers\_prefix} with the Week~1
theorem
\theoryname{sign\_verify\_mu32\_absorb\_from\_pos64}.

\paragraph{List-level adapter.}
\theoryname{challenge\_input\_eq\_after\_raw\_mu} reuses
\theoryname{challenge\_input\_eq\_from\_mu\_prefix}; together with
\theoryname{challenge\_input\_eq\_components}, this gives equality of the list
transcript whenever highbits and LSB components are also equal.

\paragraph{Local loss.}
For the proved wrapper/helper seam,
\[
  \delta_\mu=0.
\]
This is deterministic equivalence, so no statistical bad event is introduced
at this seam. The statement is deliberately not promoted to
\(\delta_{\mathsf{Sign}}=0\), \(\delta_{\mathsf{Verify}}=0\), or
\(\delta_{\mathsf{Encoding}}=0\). Exact generated top-level control,
highbits/LSB packing, the full challenge call, public memory contracts, and
paper-ROM correspondence remain open.

\paragraph{Top-down graph position.}
This theorem sits below the exact public APIs and above the Week~1 mu32 absorb
lemma. Closing \claimid{OBL-MU-TOPLEVEL-CONTROL} will make the relation an
actual raw call-site theorem; closing \claimid{OBL-HIGH-LSB-PACK} will then
permit the complete challenge-input relation.
