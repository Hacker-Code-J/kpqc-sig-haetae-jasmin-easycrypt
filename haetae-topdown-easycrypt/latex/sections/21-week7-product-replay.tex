\section{Week 7: Product/Replay Feasibility Gate}

Week~7 treated \texttt{OBL-MU-PRODUCT-REPLAY} as a bounded feasibility gate,
not as a lane to expand indefinitely. The success criterion was strict:
the theorem surface had to contain the actual generated procedures
\path{SignMuHashTarget.M._sf_mu_rawpre} and
\path{VerifyMuHashTarget.M.__verify_hash_mu}, and the post-state values
stored in the actual trace modules had to be related directly.

The desired surface remained:
\[
  \mathsf{VerifyAccept}
  \Longrightarrow
  \mu32\_prefix(\mu^{64}_{\mathrm{sign,obs}},
                 \mu^{32}_{\mathrm{verify,obs}}).
\]
The two symbols denote these post-state fields:
\begin{itemize}
\item Sign observation: \path{SignRawApiMuTrace.observed_mu};
\item Verify observation: \path{VerifyCryptolabMuTrace.observed_mu}.
\end{itemize}

The compiled prerequisites from Weeks~5--6 remain valid:
\begin{itemize}
\item actual generated returned-value hash equality on the raw branch,
\item exact actual-to-trace preservation for the sequential Sign$\rightarrow$Verify
trace, and
\item accepted Verify control and actual hash-input binding.
\end{itemize}

The missing step is a semantic transport:
\[
  \text{return-value pRHL theorem}
  \Longrightarrow
  \text{stored post-state observation equality.}
\]

The obstruction is not another memory-frame lemma. It is the gap between two
already completed trace executions and the returned values of the actual
generated hash calls embedded inside their residual continuations. The Sign and
Verify continuations are different, and Week~7 deliberately refused four
unsound escapes:
\begin{itemize}
\item adding another caller/observation wrapper,
\item assuming observation equality as a precondition,
\item adding a SHAKE locality axiom, or
\item discarding the unmatched Sign continuation without the still-open
losslessness obligations.
\end{itemize}

Concretely, the compiled pRHL goal names
\texttt{res\{1\}} and \texttt{res\{2\}} returned by the two generated hash
procedures. The unary sequential post-state names only the two module fields
\texttt{observed\_mu}. After both trace calls return, the local hash-return
variables are no longer in scope. Applying the pRHL theorem earlier leaves the
unequal residual continuations; one-sided \texttt{seq} elimination requires
the unproved Sign losslessness result. Thus the failed prototype is a program
logic surface mismatch, not an SMT arithmetic failure. Direct \texttt{sim},
\texttt{seq}, and consequence were not retried through another wrapper.

Accordingly, the Week~7 decision is:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{OBL-MU-PRODUCT-REPLAY}: \textbf{DEFERRED},
\item paper boundary: \textbf{explicit},
\item \texttt{OBL-DIRECT-OBSERVED-MU}: remains \textbf{PARTIAL},
\item no claim of $\delta_{\mu,\mathrm{raw\_api\_accept}} = 0$.
\end{itemize}
