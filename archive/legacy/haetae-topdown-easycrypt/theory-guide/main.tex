\begin{abstract}
This document reconstructs as mathematical statements the connections currently
established between the HAETAE-2 paper specification, Jasmin implementation,
and EasyCrypt proofs. The main results are a partial-correctness theorem that
the first 992 bytes of the secret key returned by every terminating internal
KeyGen execution equal the verification key, and relational theorems derived
from this invariant: the actual generated Sign and Verify procedures produce
the same 32-byte message-digest prefix and reach the same challenge-suffix
state along their raw/internal paths. Statistical loss is 0 at this local
boundary. Sequential VK/SK exports by the actual raw KeyGen caller,
integer/64-bit address bindings, exact Sign/Verify traces, successful Verify's
arrival at the tail, and hash-input bindings were also verified. The frame
condition of actual Sign output, region-local hash equivalence, and the
sequential trace of actual Sign followed by Verify were closed. However, the
product/replay edge transporting stored \(\mu\) observations from completed
traces is frozen as an explicit paper boundary.

Along the signature functional path, the work proved the 1056-byte prefix-codec
round trip under canonical inputs, HBZ prepare/apply, actual table certificates,
pure rANS steps and normalization-byte stacks, actual suffix copying, and
harness control. Week~10 added array/list suffix connections, actual W32/W64
word steps, byte/frame preservation and cursor non-underflow in the generated
inner normalization loop, leaf theorems for final 4-byte serialization, and
the actual \identifier{_rans_encode} successful size bound
\(4\le 1024-\mathsf{off}\le1024\). Week~11 installed the tail-aware invariant,
generated word-step bridge, one-symbol outer transition, and final
serialization composition inside the actual generated procedure. The resulting
success-conditioned partial-correctness theorem compiles: a terminating actual
\identifier{_rans_encode} either returns failure or, on success, its entire
returned suffix equals pure \identifier{trace_bytes}, with the offset--length
equality and initial-prefix frame. Week~12 directly closed the actual generated
\identifier{_rans_decode} initial 4-byte parsing, concrete table lookup and word
update, 0/1/2-byte normalization replay, outer/inner loop invariants, and final
rejection check. The resulting exact-trace partial-correctness theorem compiles:
a terminating execution on exact \identifier{trace_bytes} input recovers
1024 symbols, consumes the exact size, preserves the output tail, and returns
\(bad=0\). Week~13 transported the actual encoder suffix and the pointwise
region equality established by actual copying into the decoder's exact-trace
input, proving both decoder non-execution on failure and 1024-symbol recovery
on success in the same harness. Week~14 lifted the actual full-HBZ
prepare--rANS--apply boundary to the production SignaturePack/Unpack extraction
boundaries, proving preservation of the initial buffer on failure and recovery
of the original 1024 coefficients with a tail frame on success in one contract.
Week~15 showed that actual preparation of all-zero HBZ produces an all-6 symbol
sequence, and excluded actual encoder failure using a pure upper bound of
1020 bytes on that sequence's normalization budget. Thus the production
pack/unpack nonzero-size round trip compiles for terminating fixed-input
executions. This is Hoare partial correctness; it does not imply termination,
losslessness, or probability-1 success.

Week~16's 82 authored targets freeze the actual KeyGen matrix/finalizer
snapshot, actual three-helper Sign control, actual five-helper Verify sequence,
V-1/V-2/V-5/V-6, the W64 norm gate, and tail/mismatch word expressions at the
\identifier{PAPER-FROZEN} boundary. In the frozen claim matrix, KeyGen is at
\identifier{STOP-KG-NTT}, Sign at \identifier{STOP-SIGN-CHAL-MODE2}, and Verify
at \identifier{STOP-VERIFY-MATRIX-CRT}. These decisions do not establish
correctness of the three complete algorithms.

Post-freeze auxiliary proofs advanced the NTT/Rq boundary without retroactively
extending the frozen paper. Odd-root/full-NTT spectral action, mathematical
2-by-3/2-by-4 row products, and preservation of addition, multiplication, and
3-term dot-product representation from \identifier{Rq.poly} to
\identifier{HAETAE_Algebra.poly} were closed. The work also connected the nonces
515/516, SHAKE128 framing, little-endian 16-bit parsing, and \(<64513\) rejection
semantics of terminating actual mode-2 \identifier{avec} expansion to the
paper's \(a\), and composed an independent paper \(qj\) term with the finalizer
snapshot. However, compiled theorems and concrete traces confirmed that the
existing security model's synthetic \identifier{haetae_mode23_qj_vector} is a
different object from the paper's \(qj\) and actual expansion. The latest
auxiliary decision is therefore \identifier{STOP-KG-AVEC-QJ}, with
\identifier{OBL-KG-SECURITY-EXPANDVECA-SEMANTICS} as the first repair obligation.
The hint (h) codec remains deferred.
Consequently,
\(\delta_{\mu,\mathrm{raw\_api\_accept}}=0\) and
\(\delta_{\mathrm{Encoding}}=0\) cannot yet be claimed. Suffix codecs, context,
highbits/LSB, the complete challenge, retry distributions, and implementation-level
EUF-CMA also remain open. This guide is dedicated to distinguishing these boundaries.
\end{abstract}

\begin{takeaway}{One-sentence summary}
The current proofs close
\[
  \prefix_{992}(sk)=vk
  \Rightarrow
  \take_{32}(\mu_{\mathrm{Sign}}^{64})=\mu_{\mathrm{Verify}}^{32}
  \Rightarrow
  \text{equal challenge-suffix states}
\]
for actual generated raw/internal procedures, extending to actual raw-API
exports, exact traces, Sign output frames, and region-local memory connections.
Along the functional path, the actual signature-prefix codec is followed by
closed HBZ prepare/apply, actual table certificates and pure rANS steps, a
common normalization-byte trace, actual suffix copying, and 3-call harness
control. Week~10 added actual encoder inner-write semantics and successful size
bounds. Week~11 composed exact inner invariants preserving the pure tail,
generated word updates, the outer loop, and the final four stores to close the
actual encoder's successful-suffix refinement. Week~12 closed the actual
decoder's forward consumption of the same pure trace, recovering the original
symbols, exact consumed size, and output-tail frame. Week~13 composed the
failure/success branches and complete symbol recovery on success in a harness
directly calling the actual encoder--copy--decoder procedures. Week~14 composed
that result with actual full-HBZ prepare/apply and transported it to the
production SignaturePack/Unpack boundaries. Week~15 closed actual all-6
preparation, encoder-failure exclusion, and the production fixed-input round
trip for all-zero HBZ. Week~16's 82 authored targets froze the actual KeyGen
matrix/finalizer snapshot and the limited actual Sign/Verify control and
machine-word boundaries as \identifier{PAPER-FROZEN}. The frozen matrix's
stopping points are respectively \identifier{STOP-KG-NTT},
\identifier{STOP-SIGN-CHAL-MODE2}, and \identifier{STOP-VERIFY-MATRIX-CRT}.

The post-freeze auxiliary layer closed NTT spectral action, native Rq row
products, the Rq--HAETAE representation bridge, the actual \identifier{avec}
paper-\(a\) semantics, and the separate paper-\(qj\) term. Conversely, the
security model's synthetic \(qj\) was proved unequal to these two objects, so
the latest decision is \identifier{STOP-KG-AVEC-QJ}. This does not mark full
KG-1--KG-4, \(As=qj\pmod{2q}\), full KeyGen, the \(h\) codec, the complete
signature codec, or Sign--Verify correctness as complete.
The product/replay theorem connecting stored \(\mu\) observations from completed
traces remains \statusdeferred. Fixed-input termination/losslessness,
\identifier{OBL-KG-SECURITY-EXPANDVECA-SEMANTICS}, MINCORE Sign/Verify/composition,
the (h) suffix, and the complete Verify gate remain open. Verify's pure NTT
leaves are closed, but the actual 2-by-4 matrix-accumulation and CRT/freeze
procedure leaves block the combined theorem. Promotion to API/encoding zero
loss and EUF-CMA is therefore not yet justified.
\end{takeaway}
