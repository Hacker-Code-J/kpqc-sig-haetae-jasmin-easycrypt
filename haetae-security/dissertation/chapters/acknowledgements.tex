\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Acknowledgements}

This dissertation-style monograph documents the local machine-checked
\haetae{} provable-security proof surface.  It is written for technical proof
audit rather than as an institutional submission copy.

The proof development described here depends on the EasyCrypt ecosystem, Why3,
automated SMT provers, and prior work on game-based cryptographic verification,
Fiat-Shamir proofs, and lattice-based signatures.
