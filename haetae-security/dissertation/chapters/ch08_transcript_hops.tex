\chapter{Transcript Games and Erasure}
\label{ch:transcripts}

\section{Transcript Simulation}

Transcript games expose the internal structure needed for simulation and
extraction.  The proof includes modules for transcript-simulated signing,
internal transcript signing, and ROM-internal transcript signing.  These games
bridge the gap between a real signing oracle and the NMA-style games used in the
reduction.

\section{State Preservation}

Representative lemmas prove that signing oracles preserve transcript validity,
log readiness, min-entropy conditions, and ROM state invariants.  These are not
cosmetic properties; they are the invariants needed to apply later bad-event and
special-soundness arguments.

\section{Erasure Exactness}

Erasure lemmas show that internal transcript information can be removed without
changing the adversary-visible distribution.  In a paper proof this might be a
sentence.  In EasyCrypt it is a relational statement about two games with
related states and identical observable outputs.
