# Full Jasmin Port Boundary

The HAETAE scheme logic for `keypair`, `sign`, and `verify` is implemented by
Jasmin code without falling back to C.  The production library is built from
assembly generated by `jasminc`; remaining C files are test, KAT, and benchmark
harness code.

The public header exposes only `crypto_sign_keypair`, detached `crypto_sign`,
and `crypto_sign_verify`.  Signing and verification still use private Jasmin
descriptor backends internally because Jasmin's x86-64 export ABI accepts at
most six register arguments.

## Current Jasmin Coverage

The current `-DJAZZ` build uses Jasmin for:

- fixed 32-byte public-wrapper random seed generation through Jasmin
  `#randombytes`
- Keccak/SHAKE fixed byte helpers and Keccak-f1600 permutation: little-endian
  lane load/store, state zeroing, incremental absorb/squeeze, one-shot absorb,
  finalize padding, full-block squeeze, public SHAKE128/SHAKE256 state
  wrappers for init/absorb/finalize/squeeze/absorb-once/block-squeeze,
  one-shot SHAKE128/SHAKE256 and SHA3-256/SHA3-512 public wrappers, stream
  nonce byte encoding, fixed-size SHAKE128/SHAKE256 stream initializers, fixed
  SHAKE256 two-buffer and three-buffer absorb/finalize orchestration, and the
  full 24-round state permutation derived from the Formosa ML-KEM Keccak
  reference
- FFT initialization/bit-reversal, full stage-loop dispatch, per-butterfly
  complex fixed-point update, and fixed-point square-absolute helper used by
  the key-generation singular-value path
- polynomial NTT/inverse NTT and flattened vector NTT/inverse NTT wrappers
- pointwise Montgomery multiplication
- polynomial add/subtract
- low-bit extraction
- high/low-bit composition
- CRT reconstruction helpers
- vector CRT reconstruction for `polyveck_poly_fromcrt`
- polynomial `reduce2q`, `freeze`, and `freeze2q`
- polynomial high/low decomposition, vector z1 high/low decomposition,
  combined signer `z1` high/low decomposition,
  polynomial/vector composition, final challenge-polynomial comparison,
  polynomial and flattened-vector hint highbits, and VK decomposition
- simple polynomial/vector coefficient transforms: double, double-negate,
  from-Montgomery, conditional negate, conditional add/subtract by
  `(DQ-2)/ALPHA`, multiply by `ALPHA`, divide by 2, and `caddq`
- vector pointwise Montgomery accumulation, vector-by-polynomial pointwise
  Montgomery multiplication, and vector squared-norm accumulation
- vector add/subtract and vector-wide `reduce2q`, `freeze`, and `freeze2q`
- matrix-vector pointwise Montgomery multiplication for `K x L` and `K x M`
  matrices, plus `polymatkl_double` and first-column replacement used during
  public-key unpacking
- fixed-polynomial add/subtract/double/round helpers and fixed-vector squared
  norm accumulation, plus hyperball sample scaling into fixed polynomial
  vectors and combined hyperball scale-and-norm acceptance checking
- fixed-point addition, square, multiplication, high-limb scaling,
  rounded halving, signed rounded multiplication by 13-bit-shifted scalar,
  and Newton inverse-square-root iteration
- decomposed polynomial byte pack/unpack used for signature low bits
- common eta and 2-eta polynomial pack/unpack
- mode-specific q polynomial pack/unpack
- mode-specific highbits polynomial and vector packing
- public-key and secret-key packing, plus packed public/secret key byte
  decoders
- signature prefix packing/unpacking for challenge bits and `LB(z1)`,
  signature sparse-encoding size/offset validation, signature suffix byte
  layout, mode-specific full signature packing and unpacking orchestration over
  the prefix, sparse encoders/decoders, size validation, suffix
  emission/extraction, combined signature final unpack size/padding validation,
  and zero-padding validation
- mode-2/mode-3 and mode-5 challenge expansion from squeezed SHAKE bytes
- complete mode-2/mode-3 and mode-5 `poly_challenge` orchestration, including
  SHAKE256 absorb, squeeze/refill, rejection of challenge indices, and final
  coefficient placement
- packed polynomial LSB extraction
- uniform and eta rejection sampler cores after SHAKE output
- complete `poly_uniform` and `poly_uniform_eta` SHAKE stream initialization,
  block squeeze, refill, and rejection-consumption loops
- complete vector and matrix expansion loops for `polyveck_expand_vecA`,
  `polyvecmk_expand_S`, `polymatkl_expand_matA`, and
  `polymatkm_expand_matA`, using the Jasmin SHAKE-backed polynomial samplers
- complete public-key unpack/reconstruction orchestration for mode-2/mode-3
  and mode-5, including packed coefficient decoding, matrix expansion/doubling,
  mode-2/mode-3 first-column reconstruction, NTT, and first-column insertion
- complete secret-key unpack orchestration for mode-2, mode-3, and mode-5,
  composing public-key reconstruction with packed `s0`, `s1`, and key decoding
- Gaussian CDT sampling, approximate exponential, per-sample sigma-76 rejection
  arithmetic, and fixed-buffer Gaussian sample consumption
- fixed-buffer `SampleGaussN` sign-byte copy and leftover-randomness
  carry-over loops around SHAKE squeezes
- full `sample_gauss_N` SHAKE256 block/refill/sign-copy orchestration
- complete hyperball retry/control sampling for mode-2, mode-3, and mode-5,
  including Gaussian sampling, rounded inverse-square-root scaling, fixed-vector
  emission, norm-based rejection, counter update, and final one-byte
  `seed || nonce` derivation
- fixed-size SHAKE256 seed transcripts for key generation
  `SHAKE256(seed32, 128)` and signing hyperball seed derivation
  `SHAKE256(key32 || rnd32 || mu64, 64)`
- complete deterministic signature-internal and randomized detached
  `crypto_sign` signing orchestration for mode-2, mode-3, and mode-5 under
  `-DJAZZ`, including packed secret-key recovery, message digest, signing-seed
  expansion, hyperball retry sampling, challenge construction, `z` rejection,
  hint construction, signature packing retry, and the public wrapper's
  randomized signing seed. The Jasmin descriptor export records the fixed mode
  signature length after the signer returns.
- signer and verifier message-digest transcripts route directly through the
  Jasmin descriptor-based SHAKE256 three-buffer absorb and Jasmin squeeze
  wrappers
- signer fixed-point `(L,K)` rounding and first rounded `z1` polynomial copy
  used by the challenge CRT path and final hint path
- sign/verify context-prefix construction for the public wrapper APIs
- public detached `crypto_sign` and `crypto_sign_verify` wrapper orchestration
- keypair scratch vector copies into NTT form and the mode-2/mode-3 and mode-5
  matrix-vector multiplication cores, including the mode-2/mode-3 inverse NTT
  before keypair finalization
- mode-2/mode-3 and mode-5 keypair finalization passes after matrix-vector
  multiplication, including public-key coefficient finalization and secret-key
  `s2` adjustment for mode-2/mode-3
- complete key-generation singular-value orchestration for mode-2, mode-3, and
  mode-5, including FFT initialization, full FFT stage loops, square-absolute
  accumulation, top-coefficient selection, and final rounded accumulation
- complete deterministic key-generation backend and randomized
  `crypto_sign_keypair` mode orchestration under `-DJAZZ`, including seed
  expansion, matrix and secret-vector expansion, rejection control,
  mode-specific keypair arithmetic, singular-value rejection, public-key
  packing, and secret-key packing
- signer challenge-product setup for `cs1[0]`, the NTT challenge scratch, and
  the `cs1[1..] = c*s1` and `cs2 = c*s2` pointwise/inverse-NTT loops
- signer `Ay += 2*round(y2)` accumulation after matrix-vector multiplication
- signer challenge transcript construction from `Ay` and `round(y0)`,
  including hint-highbits derivation, highbits/LSB packing, and mode-specific
  challenge sampling
- signer `z = y + (-1)^b c*s` construction, both fixed-vector squared-norm
  rejection checks, and the `2z-y` scratch construction used by those checks
- signer hint construction after `z2` rounding, including doubled `z2`,
  `freeze2q`, highbits hinting, and conditional hint normalization
- verifier `z1` composition from signature high/low parts, `z1` squared-norm
  accumulation, and first-polynomial `wprime` LSB derivation
- verifier flattened NTT/matrix multiply, inverse NTT, CRT reconstruction, and
  `freeze2q` middle path before hint recovery
- verifier `w` and `z2` recovery after CRT reconstruction, including hint
  application, alpha multiplication, first-polynomial `wprime` correction,
  `reduce2q`, division by two, and final `z1/z2` norm rejection checking
- verifier final transcript tail for all modes: highbits and `wprime` LSB
  packing, `SHAKE256(vk || pre || m)`, challenge recomputation, and comparison
  against the unpacked signature challenge
- complete verifier backend orchestration for mode-2, mode-3, and mode-5 under
  `-DJAZZ`, including signature length rejection, public-key
  unpack/reconstruction, signature unpack validation, verifier matrix
  arithmetic, norm checking, and final transcript comparison
- rANS input-symbol preprocessing and range rejection for signature `h` and
  `HB(z1)` sparse encoders, full mode-specific sparse-vector encode/decode
  orchestration for `h` and `HB(z1)`, the rANS encoder/decoder state-machines,
  encoder flush path, decoder stream verification, and decoder output remapping
  for those vectors

These are exercised by `make test`, which also checks generated KAT request and
response files against `kat/PQCsignKAT_haetae_*.req` and
`kat/PQCsignKAT_haetae_*.fax`.

## Remaining Work

The current build routes the HAETAE keypair, sign, and verify scheme
orchestration through Jasmin for all three modes. Remaining non-scheme helper
coverage for future cleanup is:

- any future variable-size randombytes wrappers beyond the fixed 32-byte
  keypair/signature seeds
- variable-input SHAKE/SHA3 orchestration beyond public SHAKE/SHA3 wrappers,
  fixed stream initializers, byte/lane/finalize helpers, incremental
  absorb/squeeze, one-shot absorb, full-block squeeze, fixed and
  descriptor-based SHAKE256 multi-buffer absorb wrappers, and the shared
  Keccak-f1600 permutation
- vector and matrix operations not already covered by polynomial or flattened
  matrix dispatch

## Recommended Port Order

1. Extend the remaining variable-input Keccak/SHAKE orchestration wrappers around
   the Jasmin absorb/squeeze/permutation core.
2. Extend deterministic byte-level tests as additional pack/unpack edge cases
   are ported.
3. Port decomposition and vector/matrix arithmetic still outside the flattened
   Jasmin dispatch.
4. Keep full KAT comparison for all modes as the acceptance gate for future
   port refinements.

Each step should keep `make test` and `make kat` passing.
