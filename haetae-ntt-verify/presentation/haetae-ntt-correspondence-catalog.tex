\section*{Global Mathematical Notation}
Let
\[
q=64513,\qquad \Fq=\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z,\qquad
N=256,\qquad \zeta=[426]_q,\qquad \brev_8=\text{8-bit bit reversal}.
\]
A value of EasyCrypt type \EC{coeff Array256.t} is read as a vector
$p=(p_0,\ldots,p_{255})\in\Fq^{256}$.  A Jasmin byte array $rp$ is decoded by
\[
D(rp)_i=\left[\operatorname{to\_sint}\!\left(\operatorname{get32}(rp,i)\right)\right]_q,
\qquad 0\le i<256.
\]
The final forward and inverse specifications are
\[
F(p)_i=\sum_{j=0}^{255}p_j\zeta^{(2\brev_8(i)+1)j},
\]
\[
F^{-1}(p)_i=[256]_q^{-1}\sum_{j=0}^{255}
p_j\zeta^{-(2\brev_8(j)+1)i}.
\]
The proof uses the forward partial sums
\[
P_{\ell,s,b}(p)=\sum_{u=0}^{\ell-1}
\zeta^{(2s+1)\brev_8(u)}p_{\brev_8(b\ell+u)}
\]
and inverse partial sums
\[
Q_{\ell,s,b}(p)=\sum_{u=0}^{\ell-1}
p_{b\ell+u}\zeta^{-(2\brev_8(b\ell+u)+1)s}.
\]
The Hoare-style semantic readings are
\[
\{P\}\;c\;\{Q\}\quad\equiv\quad
\forall s.\;P(s)\Rightarrow Q(\llbracket c\rrbracket(s)),
\]
\[
\mathbf{phoare}\,[c:P\Rightarrow Q]=1
\quad\equiv\quad
\Pr[\llbracket c\rrbracket(s)\text{ terminates and satisfies }Q\mid P(s)]=1,
\]
and
\[
\mathbf{equiv}\,[c_1\sim c_2:P\Rightarrow Q]
\quad\equiv\quad
P(s_1,s_2)\Rightarrow
Q(\llbracket c_1\rrbracket(s_1),\llbracket c_2\rrbracket(s_2)).
\]
