## Baseline caveats

- The positive smoke harness leaves extended wrong-key, wrong-message, and
  tampered-signature tests disabled.
- The repository security gate can use `.eco` caches; the project-owned fresh
  replay supplies the required uncached evidence.
- The security compilation result covers the current structural model, not a
  completed paper-faithful or target-Jasmin theorem.
- Declaration and premise checkers validate bounded syntactic coverage; they do
  not establish semantic correctness or discharge open premises.
- The NTT checker compiles its stored extraction but does not regenerate it,
  and that extraction comes from a materially different Jasmin source.

  keypair/sign/verify paths. Those paths use specialized low-level Keccak
  routines, so wrapper extraction does not close `GAP-PK-FIPS202`.
